    What we collect

    Only your drink choices, a pickup time and a first name. We never ask for a phone number, address or card details. You pay at the counter.

    Bean & Brew is a made-up coffee shop for a SOFE4850 UX course project. Designed for desktop browsers (Chrome or Edge). No real orders are placed.

    Prices are for a small. Sugar and sweetener are free at the self-serve counter. Prices before 13% HST.

    Every day, 7 am to 9 pm. Orders are ready about 10 minutes after you place them.
